Build a FULL modern AI-powered mobile app called “BabyGuess AI” using React Native + Expo for frontend, Node.js + Express for backend, and MongoDB database.

APP CONCEPT:
This app is a FUN AI entertainment app that predicts whether the baby may be a boy or girl based on traditional pregnancy symptoms, cravings, mood patterns, body changes, and lifestyle-related questions.

IMPORTANT:
The app MUST clearly display:
“This is an AI entertainment prediction based on traditional pregnancy myths and patterns. It is NOT medically accurate.”

3. AI PREDICTION ENGINE
====================================

Backend creates a weighted scoring system.

Example:
- Sweet cravings increase girl probability
- Salty cravings increase boy probability
- High energy slightly increases boy probability
- Emotional mood increases girl probability

The app calculates:
- Boy score
- Girl score
- Confidence percentage

Then returns:
“Possible Baby Girl 👧”
or
“Possible Baby Boy 👦”

with confidence percentage.
